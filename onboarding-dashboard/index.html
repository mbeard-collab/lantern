Methodology & assumptions

· Cohort: every account with a Closed-Won New Business opportunity closing on/after Jun 1, 2026 — regardless of whether the Account Plan onboarding tab has been activated. The cohort grows through the year as new deals close.

· Rep filter = the Account Plan Owner (account owner). Every section above respects the selected rep.

· Current stage = first incomplete milestone in Sales Handoff → Opp Module Setup → GS Master Search Creation → GS Fullsuite Training → Post-Onboarding.

· Days in onboarding = snapshot date − the new-business close/won date (the onboarding SLA clock starts when the deal closes).

· SLA split (30 days): Sales Handoff 3 · Opp Module Setup 6 · Master Search 9 · Fullsuite Training 7 · Post-Onboarding 5. Status = elapsed vs. current stage's cumulative target (Green <70%, Amber 70–100%, Red >100%).

· Usage: core 6 modules + overall hits from CrateDB user_module_usage_daily; AI + Opp Module from feature_usage_monthly_history. Matched to accounts by CRM ID.

· Refresh: run the onboarding-dashboard-refresh skill daily/bi-daily — it re-queries Salesforce + CrateDB and rewrites the DATA block below. Snapshot fills in as CIs work the new fields.
